## Description
Jupiter's moon Europa is only about a quarter as wide as Earth, yet the ocean under its ice may hold about twice as much water as all of Earth's oceans.

Put all of Earth's ocean water (about 1.34 billion km³) into one ball and it would be about 1,370 km wide. Under Europa's ice shell, about 15-25 km thick, scientists think there is a global salty ocean about 60-150 km deep. Gather that ocean into one ball and it would be about 1,720 km wide: roughly two of Earth's ocean balls. NASA's Europa Clipper spacecraft, launched in October 2024, arrives in 2030 to study this hidden ocean.
