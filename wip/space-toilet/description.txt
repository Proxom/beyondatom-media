TITLE: How Do Astronauts Use the Toilet? 🚽

Guess first: a bag, a fan or magnets? 👇

In orbit everything is in free fall, so nothing falls into the toilet. The answer is air: space toilets use air flow to pull urine and feces away from the body.
On the International Space Station's newest toilet (the Universal Waste Management System, 2020), astronauts use a specially shaped funnel and hose for urine and a small seat for bowel movements. The air flow starts automatically when the lid is lifted.
Foot restraints and handholds keep astronauts from floating away.
And the urine is not wasted: it is pre-treated and sent to the station's water recycling system, which also recycles sweat. After cleaning, that water becomes drinking water. In 2023 NASA showed that the station can recover 98% of the water crews bring to space.
