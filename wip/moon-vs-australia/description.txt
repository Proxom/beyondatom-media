TITLE: Is the Moon Bigger Than Australia? 🌕

Guess before it lands: yes or no? 👇

Across: the Moon is about 3,475 km wide. Australia is almost 4,000 km wide from east to west, but the mainland is only about 3,180 km from top to bottom (Cape York to South Point). So Australia is wider than the Moon, but not taller.
But the Moon is a ball. Its whole surface covers about 37.9 million square kilometers, about the same as Africa (about 30.4 million) and Australia (about 7.7 million) put together: 38.1 million. By area, the Moon is nearly 5 Australias (37.9 / 7.7 = 4.9).

About the video: an original animation, drawn to scale. In the first part the Moon and Australia share one scale. In the second part the Moon's whole surface is laid flat as an equal-area map (its area is exact; the picture on it is only illustrative), with Africa and Australia at the same scale. Maps use equal-area projections.
