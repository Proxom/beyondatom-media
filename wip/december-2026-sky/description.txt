TITLE: December 2026 Sky: 4 Dates to Save 🌠

Which one will you watch? 👇

December 9: New Moon (00:52 UT). The darkest nights of the month for stargazing.
Night of December 13-14: the Geminid meteor shower peaks, one of the best and most reliable showers of the year. NASA says to expect 40 to 50 meteors per hour under dark skies. This year a thin waxing crescent Moon sets early in the evening, so it will not spoil the show. The meteors seem to come from the constellation Gemini, which rises in the evening.
December 21: the December solstice. The longest night of the year in the Northern Hemisphere (the shortest in the Southern Hemisphere).
December 24 (Christmas Eve): the Moon is full at 01:28 UT on the 24th (the evening of the 23rd in the Americas), so it looks full all through Christmas Eve night.

Times are in Universal Time (UT); your local date and time may differ.
