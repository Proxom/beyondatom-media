TITLE: What If You Stood on Mercury? ☀️

Guess first: what would you find at Mercury's poles? 👇

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun. From its surface, the Sun can look more than three times as big as it does from Earth (when Mercury is closest to the Sun), and daytime temperatures can reach about 430 °C (800 °F).
But Mercury has almost no air (only a very thin exosphere) to hold the heat, so at night the temperature can drop to about -180 °C (-290 °F). (Even so, Mercury is not the hottest planet: thick-aired Venus is.)
The surprise: in craters near the poles that never see sunlight, NASA's MESSENGER spacecraft found strong evidence for water ice (much of it under a dark surface layer).
