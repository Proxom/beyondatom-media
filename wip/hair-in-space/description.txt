Guess first: a drain, the towel or the air? 👇

In orbit, water doesn't run down your head. Loose water floats as a ball, held together and stuck to things by surface tension, so it clings to your hair.
On the International Space Station in 2013, NASA astronaut Karen Nyberg showed how she washes her long hair: a squirt package of water, no-rinse shampoo, and lots of work with a towel and a comb. The whole wash takes only a few minutes.
And the leftover water? It evaporates from her hair, the space station's air conditioning system captures it, and it is purified into drinking water.

About the video: an original animation. The astronaut, hair, water pouch, air vent and drink pouch are simplified illustrations, not the real hardware.
