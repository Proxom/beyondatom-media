## Verified facts
- Planck length = 1.616255 × 10⁻³⁵ m (CODATA 2022), NIST: https://www.physics.nist.gov/cgi-bin/cuu/Value?plkl
- Defined as sqrt(ħG/c³). CODATA 2022 paper: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2409.03787
- Still to verify with a source before use: proton charge radius (~0.84 fm, CODATA), hydrogen atom size (Bohr radius 5.29 × 10⁻¹¹ m, NIST), and wording for "below it our physics stops working" (say "current physics can't describe" — do NOT say it is proven to be a pixel of space).
