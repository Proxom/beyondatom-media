Guess before the end: how many cm taller? 👇

In space, astronauts get taller: up to about 3% of their height, often within the first few days.

Why? On Earth, gravity squeezes your spine all day. In microgravity nothing presses down, so the soft discs between the vertebrae spread out a little and the spine's natural curve straightens. One NASA astronaut went from 171 cm on Earth to 174.4 cm in space (+3.4 cm). Back on Earth, gravity compresses the spine again, and astronauts typically return to their pre-flight height soon after landing.

You do a tiny version of this every night: lying down takes the load off your spine, so you are a little taller when you wake up.
