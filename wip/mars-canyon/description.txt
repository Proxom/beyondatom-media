TITLE: How Big Is Mars's Grand Canyon? 🏜️

Guess first: 2, 5 or 10 times longer than the Grand Canyon? 👇

That dark scar across Mars is Valles Marineris, the biggest canyon in the solar system.
It is about 4,000 km long (NASA gives about 3,870 km; ESA "over 4,000 km"), nearly ten times longer than Arizona's Grand Canyon (446 km, 277 river miles). Put on Earth, it could reach across the United States from coast to coast.
In places its walls rise more than 7.6 km (25,000 ft) from the floor, more than four times the Grand Canyon's deepest drop (1.83 km, 6,000 ft) and nearly as tall as Mount Everest (8.85 km). At its deepest it goes even further down: ESA gives about 10 km.

About the video: the Mars picture is a real NASA Viking Orbiter mosaic. The bars are to scale. The cross-section uses one height scale for both canyons and Everest (the 7.6 km wall); the widths are not to scale.
