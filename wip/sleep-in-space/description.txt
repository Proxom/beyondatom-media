TITLE: How Do Astronauts Sleep in Space? 😴

Guess first: lying down, standing up or upside down? 👇

Any way they like: in orbit there is no up or down, so astronauts zip into a sleeping bag tied to the wall (or the ceiling) (on the space station, inside a small sleeping cabin), so they do not drift into anything.
The danger: without moving air, a bubble of the carbon dioxide (CO2) you breathe out can collect around your head. Astronauts can wake up short of oxygen and gasping for air. That is why the sleeping areas are well ventilated, with air blowing through them.

About the video: an original animation. The cabin, the astronaut, the CO2 cloud and the air flow are illustrations (real CO2 is invisible).

Sources:
