## Description
Guess before the end: how many days until you're taller than Mount Everest? 👇

Start at 1.70 m and grow 1 cm every second, day and night:
1 minute: 2.3 m (taller than the door)
1 hour: 37.7 m
1 day: 866 m, taller than the Burj Khalifa (828 m), the world's tallest building
10.2 days: 8,849 m, taller than Mount Everest
About 15 months: about 400 km, the height of the International Space Station's orbit
80 years: 25,246 km, almost twice Earth's width (12,756 km at the equator)

The math: height = 1.70 m + 1 cm x seconds. One year = 365.25 days.

About the video: an original animation. The person, buildings, mountains and Earth are drawn to scale with each other; the landmarks are lined up next to you for comparison (they are not really side by side). Earth is a real NASA photo.
