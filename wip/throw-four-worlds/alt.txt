Original animation, with narration. Four stacked lanes, Earth, Mars, the Moon and Pluto, each with an astronaut on the left and a distance scale on the ground, all on one shared metre scale. Under the question Same throw, 4 worlds: who throws farthest?, each astronaut throws an orange ball at the same speed and angle (36 km/h at 45 degrees) and a live counter shows how far each ball has gone. Earth's ball lands after 11.5 m, Mars's after 28.2 m. The camera zooms out as the balls fly. The Moon's ball stays up for 9 seconds and lands at 63.2 m. Pluto's ball is still in the air: same speed, more air time. Guess how far. Pluto pulls 16 times weaker than Earth. Pluto's ball lands at 162.8 m after 23 seconds, 14 times farther than on Earth, and its lane lights up green. Which world would you throw on? A follow line, then it loops.
