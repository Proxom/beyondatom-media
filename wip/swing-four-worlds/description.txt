Guess before it finishes: how long is one swing on Pluto? 👇

Same swing on every world: a 2.5 m rope, let go from 35°. Only gravity changes. One full swing (there and back):
Earth (9.82 m/s²): 3.2 s
Mars (3.73 m/s²): 5.3 s
The Moon (1.62 m/s²): 8.0 s
Pluto (0.62 m/s²): 12.9 s, about 4 times slower than on Earth

Why? A swing is a pendulum, and its timing depends only on the rope length and gravity, not on how heavy you are. The time goes with 1 / √g: Pluto's gravity is about 16 times weaker (9.82 / 0.62 = 15.8), and √15.8 ≈ 4.

The math: the short formula T = 2π√(L/g) gives 3.17 s on Earth for small swings; we solved the full swing equation for a 35° swing, which takes about 2% longer (3.25 s). No air drag, no friction, and the rope stays straight.
