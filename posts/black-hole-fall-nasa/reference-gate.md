# Reference gate: what made the million-view originals look great (written before production, 10 Nov 18:50)
Originals: "What if you got sucked into a black hole?" (~15M) and "NASA simulated falling into a black hole" (2.4M, NASA Goddard SVS 14576 visualization).
1. FRAMING: the black hole and its glowing, lensed accretion disk fill the whole vertical frame from the first second; no empty space, no UI.
2. MOTION: one continuous, accelerating first-person fall; the disk sweeps past and wraps around the camera, so you feel the plunge.
3. COLOUR/LIGHT: hot orange-white disk against deep black and the soft Milky Way; real relativistic lensing and photon rings (NASA supercomputer render).
4. PACING: a question hook in the first second, one new idea every 3-4 s, a dramatic "point of no return", then a short ending.
5. SOUND: a dark cinematic bed with rising tension and a heavy hit at the crossing; calm narration in the NASA version.
