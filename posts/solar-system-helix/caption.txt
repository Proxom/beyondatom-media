Guess before the end: how far will you travel while watching this? 👇

You never sit still. Earth circles the Sun at about 107,000 km/h (29.78 km/s).

And the Sun isn't standing still either: it carries the whole solar system around the Milky Way at about 720,000 km/h (450,000 mph). That's about 200 km every second, so the planets don't trace circles through space, they trace spirals.

Even at that speed, one lap around the galaxy takes about 230 million years.

The counter in the video adds 200 km for every second you watch.

About the video: an original animation with real NASA images. Sizes, distances and speeds are not to scale. The planets' orbits are drawn tilted to the Sun's path, and the planets keep circling the Sun (they don't trail behind it). The galaxy is an artist's concept, and the Sun's place on it is approximate.
