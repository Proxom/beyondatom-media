Guess before the end: how many tortillas did the space station crew eat in 2024? 👇

Astronauts can't sprinkle salt in space: in microgravity, loose grains would float away, or worse, clog an air vent or get stuck in a crew member's eye.

So salt and pepper fly as liquids in small dropper bottles: the salt is dissolved in water and the pepper is suspended in oil. Bread is tricky too, because crumbs float. Since 1985, flour tortillas have been the fix, and in 2024 the space station crew ate over 3,100 of them.

About the video: real NASA footage from the International Space Station. The bottle in the video is hot sauce, not liquid salt, and the floating salt grains in the first seconds are an added animation.
