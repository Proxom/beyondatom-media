Guess before the end: how fast is this room moving? 👇

This is the inside of the International Space Station, filmed by the crew on October 10, 2022, as they floated through it.

Up there nobody walks: you push off and float from module to module. The station has six sleeping quarters, two bathrooms, a gym and a 360-degree window, and its living and working space is bigger than a six-bedroom house. Astronauts work out at least two hours a day to protect their muscles and bones.

And the whole time, the station is flying at almost 8 km (about 5 miles) every second, all the way around Earth about every 90 minutes. That's 16 sunrises and 16 sunsets every day.

About the video: real NASA footage, cropped to vertical and sped up. The window at the end belongs to a SpaceX Dragon capsule docked to the station.
