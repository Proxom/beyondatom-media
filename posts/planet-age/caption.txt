How old are you on Mars? Tell me below 👇

A planet year is one full trip around the Sun. Each planet takes a different time, so if you're 20 on Earth, your age is different everywhere else.

Your age if you are 20 on Earth:
Mercury 83.0 · Venus 32.5 · Earth 20 · Mars 10.6
Jupiter 1.69 · Saturn 0.68 · Uranus 0.24 · Neptune 0.12

Neptune takes about 165 Earth years to go around the Sun once. It was discovered in 1846 and finished its first full orbit since then in 2011. So on Neptune, your first birthday would come when you are about 165 in Earth years.

Your Mars age = your Earth age ÷ 1.88.

About the video: an original animation using real NASA images. Sizes are not to scale and the clocks are sped up. The ring around each planet shows progress through one planet year (a full ring = one year). Ages use each planet's sidereal orbit period.
